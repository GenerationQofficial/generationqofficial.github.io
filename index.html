The GenQ uploader

The GenQ uploader is a small private tool that GenerationQ uses to publish its own short videos to the Generation Q YouTube channel and to read that channel's statistics. It is used only by the GenerationQ team, only on our own channel.

  • Uploads videos that GenerationQ made itself, with their titles, descriptions and publish times.
  • Reads view, watch-time and subscriber numbers for our own channel, so we know what our audience likes.
  • Does not access anyone else's account, and does not collect data about viewers.

It uses the YouTube API Services. See our Privacy Policy.
